ABYORA TRAVEL WEBSITE
======================

This is a static website ready for Netlify Drop deployment.

PUBLISH IT:
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in or create a free Netlify account.
3. Drag the extracted folder named "abyora-travel-website" onto the page.
4. Netlify will publish it and provide a netlify.app URL.
5. In the Netlify dashboard, open Forms and check that the "travel-enquiry" form is detected. Submit a test enquiry after deployment.

FILES:
- index.html: website content and enquiry form
- styles.css: visual design and mobile layout
- script.js: mobile menu and current year
- thank-you.html: form confirmation page

IMPORTANT:
- The enquiry form uses Netlify Forms. It works after deployment on Netlify, not by opening index.html directly on your computer.
- The site does not yet process payments, book airline seats, confirm hotel rooms, or query live supplier availability.
- Destination cards are inspiration only. Confirm safety, supplier agreements, dates, availability and prices before advertising or selling trips.
- Add your verified business email, phone number, licence details, privacy policy and terms before promoting the site to customers.
- Images are loaded from Unsplash, so an internet connection is needed to display them.
